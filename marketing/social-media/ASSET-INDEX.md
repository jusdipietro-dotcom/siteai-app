## Performance Metrics to Track

Monitor these KPIs for campaign success:

- **Reach**: Total unique accounts that see the post
- **Impressions**: Total number of times the post appears
- **Engagement Rate**: Likes, comments, shares as % of reach
- **Click-Through Rate**: Profile visits from posts
- **Conversion Rate**: Website visits/signups from Instagram traffic
- **Save Rate**: How often users save the post
- **Share Rate**: How often users share the post
- **Sentiment**: Positive vs. negative comments
